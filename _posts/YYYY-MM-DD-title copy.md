Kingdom Hearts: Birth By Sleep is the 6th game in the Kingdom Hearts series. After I beat the first Kingdom Hearts game I was told to play Birth By Sleep next, because despite being the 6th game release it is the first game chronologically (At least in the Kingdom Hearts -HD 1.5+2.5 ReMIX), I have also been told it only gets more confusing from here. The game follows 3 keyblade masters, Terra, Aqua, and Ventus on their journey to stop creatures made of repressed emotions called Unversed. The game delves into the beginnings of Xehanort's plan to open the door to Kingdom Hearts and his search for the seven princesses of light. The game was originally released on the PSP, and while the graphics were updated for the remaster, there's still hints of its portable origins in its gameplay. The game is divided into three separate campaigns, one for each of the characters, that take roughly 6-8 hours each (I completed Aqua's campaign first in 8 hours, and Terra's last in 6, whether this is a result of a shorter campaign or I simply got better after running through 3 times I can't quite tell). These campaigns can be further divided into worlds which take anywhere from 10 minutes to an hour to complete. The game has segmented perfectly so that no matter if you're playing in a doctor's lobby or a long plane ride, there's enough content to simply pull out the PSP and play. (I've always been fascinated by the gameplay design implemented by the era of 2010s handheld devices. As opposed to conventional consoles these devices could be pulled out and played pretty much anywhere, so gameplay had to be as easy to pick up and play as the handhelds themselves. I'm quite sad that true handheld console have died out D:). The PSP elements are also reflected in one of the worlds present in the game, the Mirage Arena.
