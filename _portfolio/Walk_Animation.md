## Heading 
Created the animation in blender using free models from blender
  The project was to make a short clip showing a walk cycle and interaction between two characters. This was the first project that wasn't animating a flour sack or ball, so it was much more difficult figuring out how to get the models to move in the way I wanted them to. Fortunately I found a video on YouTube that managed to show me the techniques I needed to achieve that. As the first freeform assignment of the semester I decided to get weird with it and I'm happy with how it turned out.
